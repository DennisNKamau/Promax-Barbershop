Our Services

At Promax Barbershop & Spa, we offer more than just a haircut. Our services are designed to keep you looking sharp, feeling refreshed, and leaving with renewed confidence. Whether you're stopping by for a quick trim or treating yourself to a full grooming experience, our skilled professionals are dedicated to delivering exceptional results.

Learn More

Hair Cut

Your hairstyle is an expression of your personality. Our experienced barbers combine skill, creativity, and attention to detail to deliver clean, stylish cuts tailored to your preferences. Whether you prefer a timeless classic or the latest trend, we'll help you achieve the perfect look.

Facial & Body Care

Healthy skin is an essential part of great grooming. Our facial and body care treatments are designed to cleanse, nourish, and revitalize your skin while helping you unwind in a relaxing environment.

Massages

Take a break from the stresses of everyday life with our professional massage services. Our therapists use proven techniques to relieve muscle tension, improve circulation, and promote complete relaxation.

Premium Hair Care

Keep your hair looking and feeling its best with our professional hair care treatments. We use quality products that cleanse, nourish, and strengthen your hair, leaving it fresh, healthy, and easy to style.

Hair Styling & Grooming

Complete your look with professional styling and grooming services designed to keep your hair neat, healthy, and stylish. We use quality products and modern techniques to give you a polished finish that lasts.

Premium Barbershop Experience

Whether you prefer a classic cut, a modern fade, or a complete style transformation, our experienced barbers deliver sharp, clean results with attention to every detail. We ensure every haircut complements your personality, lifestyle, and confidence.
